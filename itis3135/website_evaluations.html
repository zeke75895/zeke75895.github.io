Website Evaluations

YouTube

  • Contrast: YouTube excels at contrast with a clean white background, bold red accents for the logo and call-to-action buttons, and clear visual separation between the navigation header, content area, and sidebar recommendations. The bright red subscribe button stands out against neutral backgrounds, making important actions immediately visible.
  • Repetition: The platform maintains consistent design patterns throughout: repeated use of rounded rectangular thumbnails for videos, consistent typography hierarchy, and repeated navigation elements at the top of every section. The signature YouTube red branding is consistently applied, creating strong brand recognition and predictability for users.
  • Alignment: All elements align to a clear grid system with videos arranged in neat rows and columns. The sidebar, header, and main content area are precisely aligned, creating a sense of order and professionalism. This alignment makes the interface easy to scan and navigate.
  • Proximity: Related elements are logically grouped together—video recommendations are clustered, channel information is grouped near the video title, and navigation items are grouped in the header. This organization makes it intuitive to find related content and information without searching.

PNWX

  • Contrast: The website suffers from poor contrast throughout. Text colors blend into backgrounds, buttons are not clearly distinguishable from regular text, and there's no clear visual hierarchy to guide the user's eye. Important information doesn't stand out from less important information, making it difficult to identify actionable elements.
  • Repetition: There is inconsistent styling across pages and sections. Different fonts, colors, and layout structures are used without a unifying design system. This lack of repetition makes the site feel disjointed and unprofessional, and users must relearn the interface as they navigate to different sections.
  • Alignment: Elements appear scattered and misaligned without following a consistent grid system. Text, images, and buttons are positioned arbitrarily rather than aligned to a structured layout. This creates a chaotic, unprofessional appearance and makes the page difficult to scan and understand.
  • Proximity: Related elements are not logically grouped together. Navigation items are spread throughout the page, information is scattered randomly, and there's no clear visual separation between different sections. This poor organization makes it difficult for users to find information or understand the site's structure and purpose.
